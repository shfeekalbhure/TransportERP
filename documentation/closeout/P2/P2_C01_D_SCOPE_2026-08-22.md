## Phase purpose

P2-C01-D governs physical receipt after a departed trip, partial/full unload, transit holding, reallocation from a transit location, arrival finalization, operational trip closure, and read-only movement inquiry. It consumes the immutable C execution history and must never rewrite prior LOAD/DEPART evidence.

## Effective W2 actions in scope

### Mutating commands
1. `W2-P2C01-022 RecordArrival`
   - `POST /api/v1/trips/{tripId}/arrivals`
   - permission `arrival.record`
   - scope `company/receiving-branch`
   - departed trip + valid destination/stop.
   - server-accepted offline queue is allowed by contract.

2. `W2-P2C01-023 RecordUnload`
   - `POST /api/v1/arrivals/{arrivalId}/lines:unload`
   - permission `arrival.unload`
   - actual quantity may not exceed authoritative in-transit quantity.
   - accepted unload appends movement evidence and atomically updates holding balance.

3. `W2-P2C01-024 ReallocateTransitQuantity`
   - `POST /api/v1/holdings/{holdingId}:allocate`
   - permission `waybill.reallocate`
   - only AVAILABLE transit holding, quantity <= available and compatible next route.
   - previous trip/allocation/movement history remains intact.

4. `W2-P2C01-040 FinalizeArrivalReceipt` (RR1)
   - `POST /api/v1/arrivals/{arrivalId}:finalize`
   - permission `arrival.finalize`
   - all expected lines validated and required difference evidence present.
   - `DRAFT -> FINALIZED`.
   - finalizing a partial receipt must not falsely mark the whole waybill fully arrived.

5. `W2-P2C01-041 CloseTrip` (RR1)
   - `POST /api/v1/trips/{tripId}:close`
   - permission `trip.close`
   - trip ARRIVED; all manifest custody accounted; no open driver custody; no blocking exception.
   - `ARRIVED -> CLOSED`.
   - no settlement or commission posting is performed in D.

### Read-only movement inquiry included in D
6. `W2-P2C01-035 GetWaybillMovement`
   - `GET /api/v1/waybills/{waybillId}/movement`
   - permission `waybill.movement.view`.

7. `W2-P2C01-036 GetItemMovement`
   - `GET /api/v1/waybills/{waybillId}/items/{itemId}/movement`
   - permission `waybill.item.movement.view`.

These two reads were explicitly deferred from C and are included now to prove reconstructable movement/custody state across load, depart, arrival, unload and reallocation.

## D invariants

1. Actual unloaded quantity never exceeds current in-transit quantity.
2. Transit reallocation never exceeds AVAILABLE holding quantity.
3. Holding balance mutation and its accepted movement evidence commit atomically.
4. Accepted MovementEvent rows are append-only at both EF and PostgreSQL boundaries; no update/delete.
5. Partial arrival remains operationally partial while any required quantity is still in transit.
6. Finalized arrival receipt cannot be directly edited; corrections use governed compensating evidence, never history rewrite.
7. RecordArrival/RecordUnload/Reallocate/Finalize/Close are idempotent according to their W2 operation identity rules; materially different replay payloads conflict.
8. Receiving branch/location scope is resolved from authoritative server data; client-supplied IDs cannot widen scope.
9. Same-company cross-branch and cross-company access must be negatively tested.
10. Serializable/optimistic concurrency must preserve non-negative physical balances under real races.
11. Trip close requires custody reconciliation and does not perform TripSettlement, commissions, accounting posting or financial close.
12. Re-dispatch from transit creates no new Waybill revenue and does not rewrite the previous trip.
13. Movement inquiry is ledger-derived and read-only; displayed balances must be reconstructable from accepted history and cannot be manually overridden.
14. Offline clients may capture/queue only actions explicitly allowed by W2; finalization and trip close remain online-required.
