## Cross-cutting preservation rules

- Any `Move/Merge/Split/Rename/Refactor` requires before/after dependency, contract, database, runtime, test, and evidence maps.
- No sealed report or evidence artifact is edited; a later change is a new version and seal.
- `CANDIDATE FOR REMOVAL` is the strongest allowed label before consumer, preservation, and owner review.
- No proposal above permits branch cleanup, migration rewrite, data repair, or Production action.
